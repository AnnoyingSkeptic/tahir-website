About

Guitar-first.
Production-deep.

Based in Istanbul, I'm a guitarist and music producer focused on session work, original production, and audio for film and games. Jazz is my foundation — melodic phrasing, feel, and tone — but I work across styles and move between a warm guitar line and a layered production without losing the thread.

I collaborate with artists, studios, directors, and developers who need a versatile creative partner. Whether you need a session guitar track, a produced instrumental, or a full soundtrack, I can handle both the performance and the production side — remotely or in-studio.
